# C-25 -- out-of-season flowers read as a rare find, not a standing mix

What this checks: whether flowers that are out of their own flowering season
are rare. Every species has a part of the year when it flowers; outside it,
the species is "out of season". With the same short year as C-24 (Season
days 5, restart, 8x), look around the meadow at three or four different
points in the year, clicking a handful of flowers each time -- the summary
panel says whether each one is in season.

PASS: there are always some flowers in the meadow, but at every point only an occasional flower you click is out of season -- one here and there, not a large share.

If it fails, `out_of_season_seed_weight` (0.06) is lowered further; it must
stay above zero.
