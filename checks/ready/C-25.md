# C-25 -- out-of-season flowers read as a rare find, not a standing mix

With seasons on, watch a full year go by (or a headless run over the same
span). Something is visibly in flower at every point in the year, as C-17
required, but a species that is out of its own flowering window should now
turn up as an occasional find rather than as part of the standing mix the
way it did at the old 0.15 floor.

PASS: at no point in the year is the meadow empty of flowers, but an
out-of-season species is noticeably scarcer than an in-season one -- a rare
find, not a background constant.
