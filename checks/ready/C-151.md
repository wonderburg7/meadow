# C-151 -- SG-4 opening milestones: a new Colony Run shows the opening steps in order

What this checks: start a new Colony Run (main menu, Colony Run, then Start), look at the Goals line at the top left of the play screen, and do nothing else. Its text should read "Build your first cell", then "Bring in nectar and pollen" once a cell is finished, then "Let the queen lay an egg" once the stores are in, then "Raise your first worker" once the egg is laid. Each step should change within about a second of its condition holding, and a first worker should come within about ten minutes (the C-139 re-run).

PASS: the Goals line shows the four steps in that order, each changing within about a second, and a first worker appears within about ten minutes.

If it fails, the opening milestones in scripts/colony_milestones.gd (the MILESTONES list) or the one-second check in scripts/main.gd reopen SG-4.
