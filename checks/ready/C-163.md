# C-163 -- Hard soil needs Mighty Mandible to dig

What this checks: In Godot, load the saved run from the HS-2 scenario (test_scenarios/hs2_hard_soil.json), or start a new run and look at the soil from the cavity outwards. Click a soil block in the greyer outer band and try to mark it for digging with a Miner selected; then buy Mighty Mandible and try again.

PASS: The outer soil is visibly greyer than ordinary soil and different from the dark bedrock, clicking it to dig does nothing until Mighty Mandible is bought, and it can be marked afterwards.

If it fails, the colour in `scripts/soil_block.gd` (HARD_SOIL_COLOR / HARD_SOIL_SPRITE_TINT) or the marking gate reopens HS-2.
