# C-163 -- A new Colony Run: Problems star on, Goals shown, Colony window readouts filled and a Queen row

What this checks: in a Colony Run, open the Colony window (C) and untick the Problems star and close the Goals list (the Goals button, top right). Quit to the main menu and start a new Colony Run. Open the Colony window again, look at the Readouts rows and hover one, and look at the top of "Who is doing what". Change the queen's job and look again.

PASS: the new run starts with the Problems star ticked and the Goals list open, every readout row shows its value with its explanation on hover, and a "Queen: <job>" row names the queen's current job and follows a job change.

If it fails, the new-run reset in `scripts/session.gd::reset_run_state` or the readout and queen rows in `scripts/colony_panel.gd` (`_make_stat_row`, `refresh`) change; blank readouts after this fix mean the cause was not the label's size and needs a fresh look.
