# C-140 -- First crisis: can you see the heavy rain coming, tell why it hurts and answer it?

What this checks: whether a player who has not read the code can spot the
first crisis coming, work out why it hurts and do something useful about it.
In PowerShell, go to the repo root: at home, run
`cd C:\Users\david\Documents\Meadow\meadow`; at work, run
`cd C:\Users\dl13860\Documents\Meadow\meadow`. Close the game, then run this once headless:
`python tools/run_scenario.py test_scenarios/c140_first_crisis_warned.json --install-save "C140 warned"`.
If that name already exists, remove that check save in Saves and close the game before rerunning.
Open the game, then the cog menu > Saves, and load "C140 warned".
The warning says heavy rain is coming in about two minutes, and the line
under the day counter reads "Heavy rain due in ...". Carry on from there
and answer it however you think best. When the rain
arrives, press "Why?" (bottom right, above Dr Gallerin) and read "Heavy rain
keeping foragers in". Watch the stores until the line reads "Heavy rain has
passed".

PASS: before reading any code or plan you could say what was coming, why it would stop forage coming in and what you could do in the lead, and the stores and foragers behaved as the warning and the Why? entry said they would.

If it fails, FC-2's explanation reopens (`plans/archive/crisis-readiness_2026-10-17.md`,
archived once FC-2 closes): the warning text in `scripts/main.gd::_first_crisis_warning_text`,
the corner line in `_show_first_crisis_note`, or the Why? entry
`first_crisis_rain` in `scripts/guidance_catalogue.gd`. A lead too short to
act on means changing `GameConfig.first_crisis_warning_lead`. When you report
a failure, say which of the three left you guessing, and what you did in the
lead.
