# C-166 -- Short-term goals in the Goals drop-down: star, Dismiss and completion

What this checks: Start a Colony Run from the main menu and play until a short-term goal appears under the milestone in the Goals drop-down (top right; the Goals button opens and closes it). Star the goal, press Goals to collapse the list, then open it again and press Dismiss (also reachable with Tab and Enter). Later, let one finish.

PASS: A goal shows its outcome, progress and pay; the starred one stays visible while collapsed; Dismiss removes it and nothing replaces it until the next day; finishing shows a "done" line and one Dr Gallerin line, and nothing pauses the game.

If it fails, the layout or wording in `scripts/hud.gd` (short-term rows) or `scripts/colony_milestones.gd` is reopened as a follow-up to SG-3.
