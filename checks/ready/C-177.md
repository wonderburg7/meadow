# C-177 -- A selected drone has no Change to menu, and a job change leaves drones idle

What this checks: in a run that has a drone (tick "Raise a drone" in an empty nursery cell's popup and let it hatch, or load a run with one), click the drone, then drag a box round the drone and a worker together and pick another job from "Change to".

PASS: the drone alone shows no "Change to", "Doing" or fallback rows in the command tray, and after the mixed job change the worker has the new job while the drone stays a drone, idle in the hive.

If it fails, the drone guards in `scripts/hud_command_rows.gd::_rebuild_role_controls` (`_all_drones`) or `scripts/orders_zones.gd::_assign_role` change.
