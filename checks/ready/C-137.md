What this checks: on the main menu, open the cog > Appearance. With Text scale at 1, switch Typeface between Standard and OpenDyslexic and compare the settings panel's text; repeat at Text scale 2. Also look at Munro and Silkscreen: their letters should be crisp, not blurred.

Re-run after 24 Oct 2026: OpenDyslexic and Silkscreen were "slightly too small" once width-matched, so both are now drawn 6% larger.

Re-run after PN-1 (5 Nov 2026): OpenDyslexic and Silkscreen are now sized to Standard's line height instead of its width. With Typeface switched between Standard, OpenDyslexic and Silkscreen, the `<` `>` buttons beside it and the body text should stay the same height, with nothing cut off, at Text scale 1 and 2.

PASS: at each scale, OpenDyslexic's and Silkscreen's text looks about the same size as Standard's (neither visibly smaller nor wider), and Munro and Silkscreen stay crisp.
