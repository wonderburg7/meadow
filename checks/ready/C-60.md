# C-60 -- panel texture: flat, honeycomb and grain in all three themes

In the game, open Settings, Appearance, and find "Panel texture". For each of
Theme = Dark, Light and High contrast, set Panel texture to Honeycomb, then
Grain, then back to Flat, with a panel open (the Colony panel is enough).

PASS: in all three themes the panel's outer edge and every line of text stay
exactly where they were when the texture changes, the texture appears
immediately, and text is comfortably legible (in High contrast especially).

Owner: `plans/now/ui.md` (UI-Y). A failure would change the texture strength
or spread in `scripts/ui_theme.gd::_build_panel_texture`.
