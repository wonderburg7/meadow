# C-24 -- peak season reads as a good summer, not a different game

What this checks: how much more a flower gives in the middle of its own
flowering season than at the start or end of it. In Game Settings > Seasons &
health, set "Season days" to 5 (the shortest year), restart the run and play
at 8x. Click one flower and keep its summary panel (bottom left) open. Watch
the "Nectar: ... (+x/s)" figure for a minute or two as its season comes and
goes.

PASS: the +/s figure climbs smoothly to a peak and falls smoothly away again, never jumping from one value to another, and a flower in full season feels like a good summer compared with a lean spell -- clearly better, but not so much better that in and out of season play like two different games.

If it fails, `peak_season_strength` (0.75, against an out-of-season trickle
of 0.05) is lowered or the curve reshaped.
