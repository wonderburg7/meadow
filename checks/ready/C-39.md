# C-39 -- the queen keeps a full-comb nectar trip until the player makes room

Play Challenge 1 through stage 1.12, where the player is asked to build the
second comb cell (`docs/challenge-stages.md` describes the stage). Fill the
first nectar cell, then take the queen to a flower for nectar and command her
home before building the second cell. Watch her as she reaches the hive, then
build or designate the second cell and let her try again.

PASS: the queen remains at the hive carrying the nectar she gathered; neither
her load nor the stored nectar total disappears. Once the second cell is
available, she can deposit that same load. At stage 1.14, where the second
cell is reserved for pollen, a genuinely full comb instead shows Dr Gallerin's
warning that there is no room for nectar before the trip is discarded.

Owner: `plans/now/challenge-1.md` Slice 25.
