# C-57 -- Challenge 2's first stage lets a cell raise a nectar Forager, not yet a pollen one

What this checks: start Challenge 2 and, at its first stage (2.1, keep
nectar coming in -- `docs/challenge-stages.md`), click a comb cell and open
its Raises menu. Then play on to 2.2 (raise a pollen Forager) and open the
same menu again.

PASS: at 2.1 the Raises row offers "Forager..." with only "Forager -- Nectar" in it; from 2.2 it offers Pollen as well; no stage shows "Raises:" with nothing to choose.

If it fails, the 2.1 unlock in `scripts/challenge_stages.gd` or the menu's
mode gating (`hud.gd::_fill_hatch_menu`) is wrong, and a player can either
raise nothing at 2.1 or finish 2.2 before it starts.

Owner: `plans/now/ui.md`
