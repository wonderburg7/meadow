# C-36 -- a summary panel's cog fits the entity it names

Select a single bee and open the summary panel's cog. Toggle each of the
four items off and on. Then select a cell, then select a soil block. Then
box-select two or more bees and open the cog again.

PASS: for the single bee, the cog's menu shows all four items (Identity &
job, Energy & load, Activity, Trait & quote) and toggling each one hides
and shows the matching line. For the cell and the soil block, no cog
appears in the summary header at all. For two or more bees, the cog shows
only "Identity & job", and toggling it hides and shows the "N bees
selected" line.
