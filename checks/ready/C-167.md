# C-167 -- Meadow foragers face their flight direction and flap

What this checks: Start a run, send several foragers out into the meadow
(they leave through the hive door on their own), and watch them fly while
you zoom the camera in and out with the mouse wheel.

PASS: Each outdoor forager is one bee picture facing the way she flies in all eight directions, her wings flap without flicker at every zoom, and the circles come back when she goes inside.

If it fails, SP-6 reopens: the direction-to-row mapping or the wing speed in `scripts/bee.gd` (`OCTANT_NAMES`, `WING_FRAMES_PER_SECOND`) changes.
