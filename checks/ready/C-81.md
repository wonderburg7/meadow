# C-81 -- Nurse stays locked until Nurses is bought

What this checks: start level 7 from the main menu (the queen alone, no comb). Click the queen and open the Job row's "Change to…" menu; then click a built comb cell later on and open its Raises row. Once the colony can afford it, open Skills, buy Nurses, close the tree and look at both menus again.

PASS: before buying Nurses neither menu offers Nurse; after buying it both do (the one that was open when you bought it offers Nurse as soon as the tree closes).

If it fails, `Availability.role_query` is not what the menu reads (`hud.gd::_role_offered`), or the purchase is not re-applying it (`hud.gd::refresh_role_gating`).

Owner: `plans/now/brood-spine.md`
