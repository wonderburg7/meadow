# C-183 -- The queen finishes a cell she was told to build

What this checks: start a Colony Run. Select the queen, set her Job to
Forager, and in the settings window's Bee life section set "Eat below" to
0.9 so she gets hungry within a minute. Give her Build cell on an empty
tile beside the comb: she starts it, breaks off to eat, then goes back and
finishes it. Then, with "Eat below" back at 0.3, wait until she has flown
out into the meadow and give her Build cell again: she flies home, walks in
through the entrance and builds it inside the hive.

PASS: both cells are finished, and each time the queen goes back to the cell after eating or walks in from the meadow rather than going back to foraging or "building" it from outside.

If it fails, PN-4 reopens: the order kept in
scripts/orders_zones.gd (`ordered_site`) is lost over the meal, or
scripts/bee_building.gd::builder_go_to_site's entrance leg is not taken.

Runner: opus -- a timed sequence watching a moving queen across two views

Owner: `plans/now/play-notes.md`
