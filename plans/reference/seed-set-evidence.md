This reference will hold the measured summer and spring comparisons from
the ecology evidence plan. No multi-seed results have been collected yet.
PP-4's tests establish the seed-set rules; they do not establish the
size or consistency of the realised meadow change across seeds.

The implementer adds a `Summer measurements` section for EE-1 and a
`Spring measurements` section for EE-2, with provenance, per-seed tables,
reproduction commands and limitations. Keep raw run artefacts outside Git.

These accelerated, supported colonies cannot establish ordinary-run
pacing or survival. The evidence does not authorise balance changes,
offspring quality or a new ecology mechanic.
