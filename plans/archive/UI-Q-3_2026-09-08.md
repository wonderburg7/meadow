# UI-Q-3 — a message may not be cut to a fraction of its dwell

<!-- CONTEXT: archive | LAYER: provenance -->
Shipped: 2026-09-08 | From: `plans/now/ui-bottom-row.md` | Check filed: C-4

**Reasoning level: medium.** It is a rule about ordering and severity, not a
layout change, and it decides how every later warning behaves.

**Evidence (Dave, 8 Sep 2026, C-4).** Firing the three test messages eight
and two seconds apart gave dwell times of ~6s, ~1-2s and ~12s. The second —
the crab-spider escape — was cut to a fraction of the time the other two got,
because "Run saved." arrived two seconds later and took the banner.

**Delivered.** `scripts/hud.gd` gained a guaranteed minimum dwell and a
severity for the warning banner, the deferred half of UI-Q-1/UI-Q-2:

- `MIN_DWELL_SECONDS := 6.0`, plus `_warning_shown_at`, `_warning_severity`
  and `_pending_warning` fields.
- `show_warning` gained a `severity: int = 0` parameter. An arrival with
  severity no higher than the message currently showing, inside that
  message's `MIN_DWELL_SECONDS`, is held in `_pending_warning` instead of
  replacing it; a strictly higher-severity arrival still pre-empts
  immediately via `_show_warning_now`.
- `_process` promotes `_pending_warning` to the banner once the current
  message's dwell has elapsed, and a new `_dismiss_warning` (wired to the
  banner's close button) does the same when the player dismisses early.
- The existing key/gap throttle is unchanged — it solves a different problem
  (the same message repeating) and C-4 already confirmed it works.

**Check filed:** C-4 re-filed in `checks/ready.md` against the new dwell
rule — press "Fire test warnings" and confirm none of the three messages is
cut to 1-2s.
