# C1-22 — the stage's camera reaches the pollen stage

Shipped 11 Sep 2026.

## Delivery

Stage follow now routes through `camera_view.gd` in both directions. A
stage-owned queen follow rebinds to `main.queen` if a checkpoint replaces that
node; any ordinary player follow-off cancels stage ownership. The automated
foraging effect begins as the queen walks out of the hive, so the post-waypoint
focus switch starts camera and speed together at departure.

**Validation.** `tools/verify.ps1` passed: source checks, balance tests,
function map, stage reference and documentation checks. Godot 4.7.1's
headless editor scan completed without script errors.

## Original specification

**Reasoning level: medium.** Two small failures in one function, one of them
needing a live or headless observation first.

**Observable problems (C-29).** At 1.14, speed goes to 4x and back correctly,
but the camera never follows the queen. At the meadow-waypoint stage, foraging
and depositing before all three waypoints are visited can prevent the nectar
stage automation from starting.

**Established.** `_drive_c1_forage_effects()` applies camera follow and 4x
speed together, gated by `_c1_forage_effects_done` and the queen's foraging
state. The guard resets at stage entry. A direct `camera_follow_off` write
from `_run_stage_effects` bypassed `camera_view._on_follow_bee_toggled`; a
checkpoint replacement could leave an invalid queen follow target.

**Files changed.** `scripts/challenge_runner.gd`, `scripts/camera_view.gd`.

**Files not changed.** `scripts/main.gd`; `following_bee` remains the single
source of truth and no second follow target is introduced.

**Requirements delivered.**

1. `camera_follow_off` routes through
   `camera_view._on_follow_bee_toggled(null, false)`, with camera view named
   as the sole writer from the stage runner.
2. A stale stage-owned queen target re-points to `main.queen`; an invalid
   ordinary player target still clears.
3. The automatic-foraging guard resets on entry and treats the queen's walk
   out through the hive exit as setting off, while remaining one-shot.

**Invariants.** Effects start when the queen sets off, not role assignment;
follow and 4x clear at stage exit and after checkpoint restoration.

**Edge cases.** A player turning follow off by hand mid-stage is not
overridden. A queen setting off while the player is in hive view changes to
the correct view.

**Automated acceptance.** A future headless assertion will verify that at
1.14 `main.following_bee` becomes the queen within N ticks of departure, then
is null one tick after the stage completes.

**Regression check.** Stage 1.11 camera and speed continue to start and stop
at the automatic trip boundaries.
