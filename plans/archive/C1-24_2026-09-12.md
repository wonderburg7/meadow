# C1-24 — the stage marker is never left in the wrong world

Shipped 12 Sep 2026. Owner: ChatGPT Codex (Codex lane). Plan:
`plans/archive/CHALLENGE1_2026-09-15.md`.

## Original specification

**Reasoning level: low.** One missing guard, already written twice in the
same function.

**Confirmed root cause.** `challenge_runner.gd::_update_marker()` reparented
`_marker` in its 1.6/1.19 and 1.10 branches, but the 1.2/1.5/1.12/1.15 branch
set a hive-local position without restoring `hive_root`. A prior meadow stage
could therefore leave a stray yellow dot in the meadow.

**Files changed.** `scripts/challenge_runner.gd` only.

**Requirements.**

1. Establish `hive_root` for stages 1.2, 1.5, 1.12 and 1.15 before assigning
   their local marker position.
2. Hoist parent selection so each marker stage declares its intended world and
   one guard performs reparenting.

**Constraints and invariants.** `reparent(parent, false)` preserves the local
transform; all branches then assign a local position. Global positioning is
not used.

**Automated acceptance.** The headless stage-sequence assertion covers hive
parents at 1.2, 1.5, 1.12 and 1.15, and the meadow parent at 1.10.

## Delivery record

`_update_marker()` now selects `hive_root` for the six hive-local marker
stages and `meadow_root` for 1.10 before any branch assigns its position. The
single guarded `reparent(..., false)` replaces the two former branch-local
guards, so a marker left by a meadow visit cannot render at a hive coordinate
in the meadow. `tools/verify.ps1` passed (source, balance, function-map,
stage-reference and documentation checks).

**Hands-on check.** Filed as C-47 (`checks/ready/C-47.md`, indexed in
`checks/ready.md`): confirm in stages 1.12 and 1.15 that the yellow marker is
