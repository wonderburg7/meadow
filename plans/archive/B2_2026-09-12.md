# B2 — the comb index derives from the catalogue

Shipped 12 Sep 2026. Owning plan: `plans/design/architecture-review.md`
(F2). Branch `claude/B2`, merged to master.

## Original specification

Level: medium. Depends on: B1.

- **Required reading.** This heading. B1's result. `scripts/hive.gd`'s "cell
  index" section.
- **Files to change.** `scripts/hive.gd`.
- **Files not to change.** `scripts/resources.gd`, `scripts/hive_cell.gd`.
- **Requirements.** `_content_index` builds its bucket dictionary from
  `Resources.content_keys()` plus the two derived buckets, `unbuilt` and
  `ripe_honey`, rather than from a literal.
- **Invariants.** The bucket names, their contents and the `ripe_honey`
  double-membership rule are unchanged. `cells_holding()` still returns an empty
  array for an unknown key rather than erroring.
- **Implementation guidance.** Build the dictionary in a loop, then add
  `"unbuilt"` and `"ripe_honey"` explicitly with a comment saying they are
  derived, not content kinds.
- **Runtime policy.** Unchanged: the index still rebuilds only on `index_epoch`.
- **Automated acceptance.** The B1 source check now proves the two lists match
  from the other direction. Run `test_scenarios/colony_30_bees_test.json` and
  compare the summary.
- **Escalation.** None.

## What shipped

`hive.gd::_content_index` now builds its `buckets` dictionary with a loop
over `Resources.content_keys()` (the full seven-entry catalogue B1 built --
`""`, `nectar`, `pollen`, `honey`, `jelly`, `brood`, `corpse`) instead of a
nine-name literal, plus the two derived buckets, `"unbuilt"` and
`"ripe_honey"`, added explicitly afterwards with a comment saying they are
not content kinds. `hive.gd` gained a `const Resources = preload(...)`
alongside its existing `Shapes`/`HiveCell` consts, since nothing in the file
referenced the autoload-style global before.

`tests/source_checks.py::check_resource_content_catalogue` could no longer
scan a literal `var buckets := { ... }` dict for its bucket names, since that
dict no longer exists. It is rewritten to confirm `_content_index()` derives
its buckets from `Resources.content_keys()` (a regex on the `for ... in
Resources.content_keys():` line), that its two explicit derived-bucket
assignments are exactly `CONTENT_INDEX_DERIVED_BUCKETS` (`unbuilt`,
`ripe_honey`) -- no more, no fewer -- and that every `RESOURCES` row's
`"index_bucket"` still names its own `"key"`, which is what makes the
derivation cover the same content kinds the old literal did. `Resources.keys()`
still returns exactly two entries, unchanged from B1.

An equivalence run (`tools/equivalence.py test_scenarios/colony_30_bees_test.json
master`) against master showed no differences on
`status,classification,outcome,simulated.seconds,simulated.day,actions.rejected,
actions.failed,actions.unresolved,expectations.passed,expectations.failed,
process.exit_code,diagnostics.profile,diagnostics.version`. No hands-on check
filed -- nothing a player sees changes, and the acceptance is the automated
check plus the equivalence run above.

`tools/verify.ps1` is green.
