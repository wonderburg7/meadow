# HC-3 — "Change to…" looks like the buttons beside it

<!-- CONTEXT: archive | LAYER: on-demand -->

Landed 9 September 2026. Archived from `plans/now/hud-controls.md`.

**Reasoning level: low.** One property, in three places.

**What Dave reported (9 Sep 2026).** "Make the 'Change to…' button look like
the other buttons, rather than floating text." His screenshot shows Forager,
Nectar, Pollen, "Start here" and "More…" all drawn as filled buttons, with
"Change to…" as bare text between them.

**Cause.** `hud.gd::_make_role_menu()` builds a `MenuButton`. Godot's
`MenuButton` sets `flat = true` in its own constructor, and a flat button
skips its `normal` stylebox. `_style_button()` overrides all five styleboxes
on it correctly — the button simply never draws the normal one, which is why
it lights up on hover and vanishes at rest.

**Delivered, in `scripts/hud.gd`.** Set `menu.flat = false` immediately after
`_style_button(menu)`, with a comment saying MenuButton defaults to flat, in
all three `MenuButton.new()` call sites: `_make_role_menu()` (the "Change
to…" dropdown), `_make_hatch_menu()` (a role's sub-mode dropdown) and
`_build_selection_cog()` (the selection panel's field-visibility cog). The
column headers ("Job", "Focus", "Orders") are `Label`s, not `MenuButton`s, and
were left alone.

**Acceptance.** With a bee selected, "Change to…" is drawn as a filled button
matching Forager and "More…" beside it, at rest as well as on hover, and its
menu still opens on click.

**Check filed:** none. The look is proved by a screenshot in this slice's
commit, and a failure would change nothing about what gets built next.
