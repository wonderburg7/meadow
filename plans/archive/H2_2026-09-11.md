Shipped 11 Sep 2026. Owning plan: `plans/design/architecture-review.md`.

## What shipped

Comment-only. Ten `# --- section ---` headers went into `scripts/hud.gd`,
covering the 1,293 lines that carried none (F14's finding): signals, role
controls, shared layout constants, fields, problem strip, `_ready` and
construction, speed controls, the Challenge goal readout, the bottom row and
the command tray -- each placed at an existing top-level boundary (a blank
line before a function, var or const block), never inside a function body.
Six more went into `scripts/main.gd` above its first existing header at line
583: signals, preloads and boot constants, delegate systems, world and
meadow constants, state, and `_ready` and construction.

`FUNCTION_MAP.md` was regenerated afterwards so the new headers appear in it.

## Why the boundaries don't exactly match the plan's eight named regions

The plan named eight natural boundaries (signal block, `_ready` and
construction, the command tray, the role controls, the bottom row, the speed
controls, the Challenge goal readout and the problem strip). Two of them --
"role controls" and "speed controls" -- are not single contiguous blocks in
the source: `hud.gd`'s role-menu data (`hatch_options`) and its speed-preset
data (`SPEEDS`, `PAUSE_TIP`) are interleaved statement-by-statement (role,
speed, speed, role) in lines 125-182, and the top-right bar's build function
(`_build_top_right_bar`, headed "speed controls" here) also builds the
view/colony/nursery/foraging/winter buttons alongside the speed buttons,
because the source builds them together in one function. Splitting either
would mean reordering code, which the slice's own invariants forbid ("do not
reorder anything"). Per the slice's Escalation clause, this is recorded here
rather than acted on: the ten/six headers chosen are the closest honest
match to the plan's eight names at boundaries that already exist in the
code, not a claim that every header is topically pure.
