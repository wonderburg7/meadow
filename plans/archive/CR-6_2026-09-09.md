# CR-6 — make headless evidence bounded and causal

Delivered 2026-09-09.

The foreground headless runner now launches Godot through one synchronous,
bounded wrapper. Each invocation records a typed `HeadlessRunOutcome`, keeps
its output in a unique artefact directory, captures the Godot PID and stdout/
stderr, and kills/reaps the process tree on timeout. Verdict JSON now
distinguishes pass, product failure, environment failure and timeout.

The deterministic `bee_movement_smoke_test.json` is the baseline gate. On the
delivery baseline it completed within the 60-second limit, but Godot exited
`0xC0000005` before writing a session summary; the runner recorded that as an
environment failure and left no process behind. Consequently SB-1 remains out
of the queue until that Godot failure is diagnosed or repaired.

Validation: the focused headless Python suite (20 tests) and
`tools/verify.ps1` passed.
