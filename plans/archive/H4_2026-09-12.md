# H4 — a headless run can start a Challenge and reach a stage

Shipped 12 Sep 2026. H4 lets a `--scenario` run name Challenge 1 or 2, uses
the normal `debug_advance_stage()` transition seam to reach a named stage, and
exposes the current stage id to scenario conditions and assertions. Its
acceptance fixture is `test_scenarios/h4_challenge1_stage_advance_test.json`.

Level: medium. Depends on: C1-21 and C1-22 having landed. Dave decided on 11
Sep 2026 that H4 lands before C1-24 and C1-26.

**Why it existed.** Nine open slices stated a headless Challenge assertion as
their acceptance, but scenario launches used `Levels.SCENARIO_LEVEL_ID`, so
`challenge_runner.start()` never fired. There was no Challenge scenario verb,
fixture, or dynamic stage assertion.

- **Files changed.** `scripts/main.gd`, `scripts/scenario_actions.gd`,
  `scripts/challenge_runner.gd`, `docs/headless-run-contract.md`, and
  `test_scenarios/h4_challenge1_stage_advance_test.json`.
- **Requirements met.** A scenario may name a Challenge; the runner starts
  through its normal launch path; `advance_challenge_stage` reaches a stage
  through `debug_advance_stage()` without assigning `_stage_index`; and
  `challenge_stage_id` works in both `when` clauses and assertions.
- **Invariant.** A scenario with no `challenge` field keeps the existing
  synthetic scenario level. The new surface is developer/test-only.
- **Validation.** `tools/verify.ps1 -Quiet` passed: source checks, balance
  tests, function map, stage reference, and doc checks. The new fixture was
  launched once but the local Godot process crashed before writing a session
  (`0xC0000005`, no engine output), so it produced no product verdict.
- **Next.** C1-24 — the stage marker is never left in the wrong world.
