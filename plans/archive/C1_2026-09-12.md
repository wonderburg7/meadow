## Original specification

Level: medium. Depends on: A2, A3.

- **Files changed.** A new `conditions.gd` in `scripts/`,
  `tests/source_checks.py`, and a new `tests/` case file.
- **Requirements.** A static `met(main, spec) -> bool` and a
  `progress(main, spec) -> Dictionary` returning `{current, target}` or empty.
  The supported keys are `day_at_least`, `role_count`, `hive_total`,
  `cell_count`, `built_cells_at_least`, `brood_cells_at_least`, `queen_alive`,
  `event_count`, `disease`, `all_of`, `any_of`, and `custom`.
- **Invariants.** The evaluator reads but never writes game state. Role counts
  come from `main._role_counts()` and stock from `hive.total()`; unknown keys
  report an error and fail.
