# HCB-5 — a cell's outline takes the colour of what is in it

Shipped 12 Sep 2026. The implementation centralised the designation-ring
palette in `hive_cell.gd` and uses it for a built cell's contents outline;
unknown content continues to use the previous brown fallback. C-45 is the
pending hands-on confirmation.

**Symptom (Dave, 30 Aug).** With Cell art on, a forager appeared to be
contributing to a cell but the cell's look did not change until later.

**Cause, confirmed from the code 11 Sep 2026 -- there was no lag.**
`hive_cell.gd::add_resource` sets `content_type = resource_type`
synchronously on the deposit, and the presentation gate redraws at
`REFRESHES_PER_SECOND` 6 with a 0.85-1.15 jitter. At worst this is about
190 ms. `outline.visible = content_type != ""` already turns the outline on
for any amount at all. What was missing was a contents colour rather than
timing or the outline itself: it was one brown, `Color(0.35, 0.28, 0.12)`,
whatever the cell held.

**Dave's answer, 11 Sep 2026.** "Cells with any amount of a particular
resource should use the outline of that resource."

**Requirements delivered.**

1. A built cell holding a mapped resource draws its outline in that resource's
   colour; an empty cell remains outline-free.
2. The outline and designation ring read one colour lookup. The ring retains
   its own alpha while sharing the hue.
3. A content kind without an outline colour, such as corpse or jelly, falls
   back to the previous brown rather than losing its outline.

**Constraints preserved.** Unfinished sites retain their own growing-outline
treatment. `Session.use_cell_sprites` and `_apply_outline_points` continue to
own outline shape; the change only sets colour. `resources.gd` and
`ui_theme.gd` were not changed.

**Architecture note.** The lookup is explicitly marked for architecture
review B1's future resource catalogue, so B1 can move it without creating a
second definition.

**Acceptance.** A forager depositing into an empty cell makes that outline
visible at a glance; nectar and pollen cells use distinct outline hues; an
empty cell still has no outline.
