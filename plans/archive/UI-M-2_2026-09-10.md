# UI-M-2 — a hexagon's title fits inside it, at every text size

**Shipped 2026-09-10** on `claude/UI-M-2`. Kept here for provenance only;
`plans/now/ui.md` no longer lists this slice.

## What shipped

`main_menu.gd::_make_hex_tile`'s title label now:

1. Uses `theme_type_variation = "Tiny"` instead of `"Small"` -- the smaller
   of the theme's two smallest named sizes, chosen over a one-off computed
   font size so the label stays on `UiTheme`'s shared Theme resource and
   keeps tracking `Session.text_scale`/`ui_typeface` live with no extra
   recompute code. `ui_theme.gd` was not touched, as the plan required.
2. Sets `clip_text = true` as the safety net requirement 1 asked for.
3. Widened its box margins from 10px to 22px each side (bbox.x - 44 wide,
   still bbox.y - 16 tall, still centred) so the label's right edge stops
   exactly at the completion tick's left edge instead of reaching under it,
   satisfying requirement 4 by construction rather than by estimate.
4. All seven `STAGES` titles lost their punctuation after the number
   (colon after Challenge numbers, dash after Scenario numbers), per
   requirement 3.

**Evidence for the font-size choice (requirement 2).** Headless Godot
(`Godot.exe --headless --script`, and a real `Label`/`UiTheme.active_theme()`
node built the same way `_make_hex_tile` builds one) measured every title in
`STAGES`, wrapped at the label's own box width, at text scale 1.0 and 2.0:
"Small" (13pt base) overflows the box for the longest Challenge title
("Challenge 2 Brood, Roles and Expansion") at scale 2.0 by about 15px;
"Tiny" (11pt base) fits every title at every scale up to 2.0, with roughly
5px of headroom on the worst case. That measurement is not committed as a
test (see below); it was a one-off local run, deleted afterwards.

**Automated acceptance check not built.** The plan's "Automated acceptance
checks" section called for a LAY-6 `control_rect` fixture entry over the
pre-run menu. `scripts/scenario_actions.gd::_resolve_scoped_path` only
reaches `hud`/`selection_panel`/`command_tray`/`gallerin_panel` -- an
in-run HUD scene -- and `tests/fixtures/layout_panels.json`'s fixture
configures and starts a live colony run, which skips the main menu
entirely (`main.gd::_apply_scenario_boot_arg`'s comment). Reaching the
main menu through that harness needs a new scoped root (the menu already
`add_to_group("main_menu")`) and a way to open it without starting a run,
which is new scenario-runner plumbing, not a one-file change. Filed
instead as a hands-on check, C-40 in `checks/ready.md`, per the plan's own
"Completion and documentation updates" line.

## Original specification

**Reasoning level: low.** One label box and two strings, in one file, behind
a clear interface.

**Purpose and intended outcome.** Every Challenge and Scenario tile on the
pre-run menu shows its whole title inside its own hexagon, at every text-size
setting, and the titles read the way Dave wants them to.

**Observable problems (Dave, 10 Sep 2026).**

- C-33, otherwise a pass: "there's some text spilling over in the main menu
  hexagons."
- A separate ask on the same screen: "In the main menu, in the scenario and
  challenge titles, remove the dash after the scenario number, and remove the
  colon after the challenge number."

**Confirmed root cause.** `main_menu.gd::_make_hex_tile` gave the tile's
label a fixed pixel rectangle while the hexagon does not scale with the
text-size setting but the label's text does (`theme_type_variation =
"Small"`, scaled by `ui_theme.gd` from `Session.text_scale`). The label had
no clipping, so once the wrapped text was taller than the box it painted
outside the hexagon. The longest title, "Challenge 2: Brood, Roles and
Expansion", was the first to go.

**Requirements.**

1. The tile's title never paints outside its hexagon (`clip_text = true`).
2. The title fits rather than being clipped, at the default and the largest
   text scale, via a smaller type variation or a scaled-down font -- pick one
   mechanism and say which in a comment.
3. Titles lose their punctuation after the number, all seven `STAGES`
   entries.
4. The `✓` completion tick keeps its corner and is not overlapped by a
   wrapped title at the largest text scale.

**Constraints and invariants.** `HEX_SIZE`, `HEX_STEP_X` and
`HEX_BORDER_OVERLAP` did not change. `scripts/ui_theme.gd` was not touched.

**Regression checks.** C-21's condition (leftmost hexagon of both strips
unclipped, every tile selects its own stage) and C-16's left-pane inset --
neither changed; this slice never touched `HEX_SIZE`, `HEX_STEP_X`,
`hex_hit_area.gd` or the left pane.
