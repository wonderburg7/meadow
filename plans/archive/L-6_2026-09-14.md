# L-6 — two lanes closing out at once is a test

Shipped 14 September 2026.

**Objective.** State the concurrency contract as a test, over the five cases
that actually happened.

**What shipped.** `TwoLaneCloseoutTest` in `tests/test_source_checks.py`,
six tests over a fixture that claims one `[Claude]` and one `[Codex]` item
(plus a third, unclaimed anchor item -- Git's merge cannot cleanly resolve
two adjacent-but-different deletions with no unchanged line between them,
which a real queue's floor of three items never hits but a bare two-item
fixture always did):

1. `test_two_lanes_close_out_independently` -- both lanes claim, deliver and
   finish in sequence; both merge cleanly, both worktrees/branches/ledger
   rows are gone, and the queue is refilled twice.
2. `test_simultaneous_claims_one_wins_the_lock` -- a held lifecycle lock
   makes a concurrent `start` exit non-zero with the busy message; retrying
   after the lock releases succeeds.
3. `test_one_lane_finishing_leaves_the_other_untouched` -- finishing one
   lane leaves the other's worktree, branch and ledger row untouched, and
   its own `finish` later succeeds with no rebase underneath it.
4. `test_remote_ahead_integrates_or_fails_naming_remote_state` -- an
   unrelated commit pushed directly to the bare remote makes `finish`'s
   single push fail with Git's own rejection message (naming the remote
   state), and `resume` afterwards still finds and completes the lane
   rather than reporting it already done.
5. `test_dirty_coordination_file_refuses_before_the_merge` -- a real defect:
   nothing refused an uncommitted edit to `plans/workstreams.md` or
   `checks/ready.md` in the primary checkout before merging. Fixed in
   `Invoke-SliceFinish` (`tools/slice.ps1`): a `git status --porcelain`
   check over `Get-LifecycleProducts`' files runs after the primary-branch
   check and before the merge, throwing and naming the dirty file(s)
   without touching HEAD. The test also exercises the conflict Dave's 12
   Sep decision allows -- both lanes refilling `NEXTSTEPS.md` on their own
   branches and merging in sequence, over the queue file rather than the
   ledger -- and asserts `resume` still completes either way.
6. `test_resume_after_push_succeeded_and_cleanup_failed` -- injecting
   failure after the `pushed` phase, `resume` still reaches the same end
   state as an uninterrupted run, with local and remote `master` equal.

**Production fix.** One: `tools/slice.ps1`'s `Invoke-SliceFinish` gained the
dirty-coordination-file guard above (case 5). Every other case passed
against L-5's transaction unchanged -- the risk this slice priced as "high
to discover, low to land" held.

**Not a defect.** A fresh worktree's headless Godot boot check fails on
missing `.godot` import cache (`.gitignore`d); this is the known false
alarm L-8 classifies as `import_cache_missing`, not a regression here.

**Expected improvement.** The 10 lifecycle-busy collisions and the 5 queue
drift refusals `docs/agent-workflow-review-2026-09-12.md` counted are now a
covered contract instead of a surprise.
