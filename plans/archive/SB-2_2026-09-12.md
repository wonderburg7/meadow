# SB-2 — out-of-season species become genuinely rare (shipped 12 Sep 2026)

Archived from `plans/now/season-balance.md`. This was the plan's last open
slice; the plan is now complete.

**Reasoning level: low.** One value, one existing formula.

**Evidence (Dave, 8 Sep 2026, alongside C-17 passing).** C-17 passed in full:
"every season retains visible flowers, out-of-season flowers remain rare
rather than absent, and panel text/rates agree." The one note: "Out-of-season
plants should be more rare."

**Cause.** `scripts/flower_species.gd::season_weight()` (~line 185) floors an
out-of-season species' seeding weight at `GameConfig.out_of_season_seed_weight`,
was 0.15 — a species out of its window still seeded at fifteen per cent of
its usual rarity weight, which over a whole meadow read as a steady presence
rather than an occasional one.

**Delivered.** `scripts/game_config.gd`'s `out_of_season_seed_weight` lowered
from 0.15 to 0.06, with an updated comment explaining the change and why it
must stay above zero. `season_weight()`'s shape, `out_of_season_strength` and
every species' own `weight` were left untouched, as specified — a single
dial.

**Simulated before shipping.** SB-1's 60-day fixture
(`test_scenarios/foraging_evidence_base.json`) was run at both 0.15 and 0.06,
same seed, with `environment.flowers_by_species` snapshots every 3 sim-days
across the full 60-day year (SB-0's field, added for exactly this). At both
values every one of the six species (Clover, Borage, Foxglove, Poppy,
Bramble, Dandelion) appears with a non-zero count at some point across the
year — none reaches zero for the whole year at either value, so 0.06 does
not push any species to extinction that 0.15 already protected. Individual
snapshots show a species briefly absent while out of its own flowering
window, in both runs alike — that is the mechanism working as designed, not
the value under test.

**Acceptance.** Play or headless-watch a full year with seasons on. Something
is visibly in flower all year, as C-17 required, and an out-of-season species
now reads as an occasional find rather than as part of the standing mix.

**Check filed:** C-25, which re-runs C-17's condition against the new value.
