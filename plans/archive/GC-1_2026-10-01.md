## GC-1 — an Idle bee stays where she is

Level: medium (a simulation change with an acceptance check).

**Now.** `bee.gd::_apply_role` sends a bee inside the hive to
`State.IN_HIVE_TO_EXIT` for every role `BeeStatus.works_indoors` rejects,
Idle included, so she walks out and parks on `IDLING` in the meadow (review
probe C: 15 s after Idle she was in the meadow). There she can never eat:
`bee_survival.gd::should_go_and_eat` feeds only a bee inside the hive, and
`after_feeding` walks every non-indoor role back out. From full energy she
starves in about 590 s (`energy_capacity` 100 / `energy_burn_rate` 0.17),
under three game days. `roles.gd`'s Idle tip says "They stay where they are".

**Change.**

- `scripts/bee.gd::_apply_role`, in its `if _is_inside_hive():` branch:
  `state = _default_state_for_role() if (now_indoors or new_role == Role.IDLE) else State.IN_HIVE_TO_EXIT`.
  The meadow branch is unchanged, so an Idle bee outside stays outside.
- `scripts/bee_survival.gd::after_feeding`: the first condition becomes
  `if BeeStatus.works_indoors(bee) or bee.role == Role.IDLE:`, so an Idle bee
  returns to `IDLING` where she ate instead of walking out.
- `test_scenarios/gc1_idle_stays_test.json`: `"cells": 7`, no foragers,
  `"nurses": 1`, no builders or miners, `"weather": false`,
  `"disease": false`, `"rival_hive": false`, `"winter": false`,
  `"random_seed": 4242`. Actions: `set_cell_contents` nectar 200 into
  `built_index` 0, 1 and 2; `settle` 30 ticks; `assign_role`
  `{"current_role": "nurse", "count": 1}` to `"idle"`; then two asserts, each
  `"when": {"day_at_least": 5}`: `{"event_count_at_most": {"type": "death",
  "subtype": "starvation", "count": 0}}` and `{"population_at_least":
  {"role": "idle", "count": 1}}`. `"stop": {"when_actions_resolved": true,
  "max_days": 6}`. Both keys are ones the harness honours
  (`scenario_actions.gd::_legacy_when_spec`). Founders' ages are spread over
  half a lifespan (`starter_age_spread` 0.5), so no bee dies of age by day 5.

**Acceptance, from the player's seat** (file it with `python
tools/new_check.py --name "An Idle bee stays in the hive" --owner
plans/now/gameplay-consistency.md`): start Scenario 4, click a nurse in the
hive and choose Job, Idle. PASS: she stays in the hive, still there and alive
three game days later. A failure means Idle still walks a bee out to starve.
