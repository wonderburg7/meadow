# HC-4 — the default text size goes up by 2px

<!-- CONTEXT: archive | LAYER: history -->
Status: complete | Landed: 2026-09-09 | Owner: Dave / Claude Code

Delivered from `plans/now/hud-controls.md`.

**Reasoning level: low.** Four numbers in one dictionary.

**What Dave asked (9 Sep 2026).** "Increase the default text pixel size by 2."

**Delivered, in `scripts/ui_theme.gd`.** `TYPE_SIZES` raised by 2 each:
Heading 16 → 18, Body 13 → 15, Small 11 → 13, Tiny 9 → 11.
`Session.text_scale` multiplies these, so every scale setting moved with
them, and `refresh()` re-applies to open panels without further change.

`GallerinPanel.BUBBLE_TEXT_SIZE` (`scripts/gallerin_panel.gd`) was also
raised, 13 → 15, alongside the dictionary: its own comment declares it
matches `UiTheme`'s Body size, and it is a separate literal `TYPE_SIZES`
does not drive, so it would otherwise have gone stale the moment Body
changed.

**Re-measured the three widths chosen against the old sizes**
(`UiLayout.SELECTION_TRAY_MIN_WIDTH`, `GallerinPanel.BUBBLE_WIDTH`, the
100px column minimum in `hud.gd::_build_tray_column()`). None needed
raising: every label the selection tray and Dr Gallerin's bubble show uses
`AUTOWRAP_WORD_SMART`, so bigger text adds lines rather than clipping or
overflowing sideways, and `hud.gd::_apply_even_column_widths()` grows each
tray column to its content's own minimum width rather than truncating it at
the 100px floor.

**Acceptance.** At the default text-scale setting every label reads visibly
larger than before. At 800x600, nothing in the summary panel, the command
tray or Dr Gallerin's bubble clips or wraps mid-word — confirmed by
inspection of every text-bearing control's wrap/sizing mode in the three
owning scripts; hands-on confirmation at both text scales is filed as C-33
in `checks/ready.md`.

**Check filed:** C-33, `checks/ready.md`.
