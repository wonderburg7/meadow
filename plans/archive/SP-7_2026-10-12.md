# SP-7 -- the whole comb drawn from Dave's cell art

Status: shipped 2026-10-12 | Owner: Dave / Claude Code | Plan: `plans/now/sprites.md`

Dave drew honey, royal jelly, larva, unfermented and ripe bee bread,
fermented nectar and an eight-stage wax cap (10-11 Oct 2026). Decisions
(Dave, 12 Oct 2026):

- **Levels.** A cell's top frame is the full cell (bound 100, the frame
  below ends at 99), and the other frames share the rest equally, so a
  steady fill changes frame at a steady rate. Nectar and pollen moved to
  the same rule. The larva's nine stages share development equally,
  because at 100 % the bee emerges. The bounds are in
  `assets/sprites/SPRITES.md`.
- **Caps** go on stage by stage while sealed bee bread ripens, cover it
  whole once it is ripe, and come off when the bees first take from it,
  which shows the fermented art. A cell the player closes wears the whole
  cap (no animation), and the flat-look bar stays only for a cell still
  filling towards its close. Honey and brood are not capped.
- **Fermented nectar** is Dave's own drawing now, not a recolour variant.
- **Translucency** is allowed (`plans/design/pixel-art.md`): honey is 80 %
  opaque and the cap's thin middle 26-47 %.

`hive_cell.gd` picks the sheet in `_refresh` from `CELL_ART`, draws the cap
with a second sprite (`cap_sprite`, `_show_cap`), and tints a starving
larva red as the flat brood colour does. What the art does not show yet:
honey's wet-to-ripe shift (the label still shows it), and a gyne's or
drone's brood looks like a worker's.
