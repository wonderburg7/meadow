**What Dave asked (9 Sep 2026).** "Make it so the text in Dr G's speech
bubble appears one character at a time to simulate her talking."

**What shipped.** `TYPE_CHARACTERS_PER_SECOND := 45.0`, `_typing_target` and
`_typed_characters` on `GallerinPanel`. `_show_bubble()` still sets
`_bubble_label.text` to the whole line first, so the bubble is laid out and
sized at its final size before any typing starts, then resets
`_typing_target`/`_typed_characters` and `visible_characters = 0`.
`_process()` advances `_typed_characters` by
`TYPE_CHARACTERS_PER_SECOND * unscaled_delta`, where `unscaled_delta` divides
`Engine.time_scale` back out of `delta` -- Godot multiplies the delta
reaching `_process()` by `Engine.time_scale` (the game-speed control in
`camera_view.gd`), so without the divide 4x game speed would have typed the
prompt 4x faster. A shared `_finish_typing()` helper sets
`_typed_characters` to the target length and `visible_characters = -1`; it is
called both by a `gui_input` handler on the bubble (left-click finishes the
line) and by `_show_bubble()` itself before it swaps in a new line, so a
still-typing line is never left half-revealed under the one that replaces
it.

**Verification.** `tools/verify.ps1` green. Hands-on: C-38 in
`checks/ready.md`, covering the typing, the 4x-speed case, the
final-size-from-the-first-frame requirement and click-to-finish.
