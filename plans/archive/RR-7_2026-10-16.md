## RR-7 -- why Silkscreen at text scale 2 still splits a menu hexagon's word

Level: high (ambiguous bug). C-99 failed on 15 Oct 2026 (Sonnet; an Opus
re-run interrupted after one screenshot saw the same): at text scale 2 with
Silkscreen, the Challenge 1 and 2 hexagons show "CHALLENG" with "E 1" on
the next line. Not yet diagnosed.

**True now** (verified 15 Oct 2026 against master `e3e7adc`).
`main_menu.gd::_fit_hex_label` runs on the label's `draw` signal, keyed on
font instance, base size and available size, and calls `hex_fit_size`,
which steps down from `base` until `hex_text_fits` (each word's
`Font.get_string_size` within the width, and the wrapped block within the
height) or returns `HEX_MIN_FONT_SIZE` (6) whether or not that fits.

**The one question.** At text scale 2 in Silkscreen, for "Challenge 1"'s
title: does `hex_fit_size` return a size at which `hex_text_fits` is true
but the rendered Label still breaks the word (a measurement mismatch, e.g.
the Label's own spacing or oversampling), or does no size from `base` down
to 6 fit, so the floor is returned (the floor)? Answer with the printed
figures, as a line here starting `RR-7 answer:`, then specify the fix
slice below it, with its own `queue` block. Do not change game code in this slice.

RR-7 answer: neither -- the fit is right, but the label is never repainted at it. `python -m unittest tests.test_rr7_silkscreen_fit` (16 Oct 2026, headless and windowed alike, Godot 4.7.2): available 124 x 129.5, base 22, `hex_fit_size` returns 20, `hex_text_fits` true at 20 ("Challenge" measures 122.00 by `get_string_size` and by `TextLine`), and the Label lays out 3 lines at 20 (Challenge / 1 First / Stores), no word split. At 22 and 21 "Challenge" measures 134 and 128, so the paragraph breaks "Challeng / e 1 First / Stores" -- C-99's exact picture. A probe on the label's `draw` signal, connected ahead of the menu's fit, shows every settings change gives the label **one** draw, and that draw paints the size in effect *before* the fit: walking C-99's route (scale 1 then 2, each typeface in list order), Silkscreen at scale 2 paints at 22 (split), OpenDyslexic at scale 2 paints at 22 ("Challen / ge 1", split, its fit is 17), and Munro after it paints at OpenDyslexic's 17. The cause: `_fit_hex_label` runs on the `draw` signal and sets the font-size override there; the `queue_redraw()` that override triggers is dropped, because a CanvasItem ignores redraw requests until its current draw has finished, so nothing paints the fitted size until some unrelated redraw. RR-5's and HO-15's tests compare the override, not the painted size, which is why they pass.
