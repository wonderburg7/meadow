# UI-T-3 — cell highlight rings sit two pixels high

**Shipped:** 2026-09-08

**Reasoning level: low.** One constant and one line, behind a clear
interface.

**Symptom (Dave, 8 Sep 2026, C-9).** Everything else in C-9 passed: all five
rings trace the cup sprite's silhouette, turning Cell art off reverts every
ring to the flat hex within a fraction of a second, and the sprite-aligned
hex grid works on restarting the level. The one correction: "move the cell
highlight rings 2 pixels lower. otherwise as intended."

**Cause.** `scripts/hive_cell.gd::_silhouette_point_to_local` deliberately
reuses the sprite's own placement arithmetic so a ring can never drift from
the sprite. The rings were two pixels high because the measured
`EMPTY_SILHOUETTE` sits two pixels above where the drawn cup reads, not
because the arithmetic disagreed with the sprite.

**Delivered.** Added the ring-only `RING_Y_NUDGE := 2.0` after the sprite's
own y-placement arithmetic. `EMPTY_SPRITE_Y_NUDGE`, `_ready()`'s
`empty_sprite.position`, and the flat-hex `_set_outline_points` path remain
unchanged.

**Acceptance.** With Cell art on, the base outline, designation ring,
selection ring and nursery glow/halo sit two pixels lower and read as centred
on the cup. Cell art off is unchanged.

**Check filed:** C-20 in `checks/ready.md`.
