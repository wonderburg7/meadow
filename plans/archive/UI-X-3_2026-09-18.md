# UI-X-3 — a comb cell's tray uses three columns

Delivered 18 Sep 2026. Was `plans/now/ui-x.md`'s UI-X-3 section.

**Purpose.** The bee half of C-18 passed. For a comb cell the tray shows two
columns, and Dave wants three so it uses less height. **Decision (Dave, 13 Sep
2026, in session):** split Nursery into two columns, giving *Reserve for |
Priority | Raises*. More… (Emptying, Sealing) is unchanged.

**Root cause, confirmed.** `_build_cell_group` makes two
`_build_cell_action_column` calls into `cell_primary_columns`: "Reserve for"
and "Nursery". `_build_nursery_controls` stacks Priority, Open nursery window,
Raises, Keep for every bee and the gyne row in one column, and that stack is
the tray's height. `_update_command_tray_width` already runs
`_apply_even_column_widths(cell_primary_columns.get_children())`, so a third
column gets an equal share with no further change.

**Delivered.** `scripts/hud.gd::_build_nursery_controls` split into
`_build_priority_controls(column)` (the Priority row and Open nursery window)
and `_build_raises_controls(column)` (the Raises row, Keep for every bee, and
the gyne row), and `_build_cell_group` now builds three columns -- "Reserve
for", "Priority", "Raises" -- into `cell_primary_columns`. Field names
`hatch_row`/`lock_row`/`gyne_row` and the `live_buttons` keys are unchanged.
`test_scenarios/uiw2_challenge2_raises_forager_test.json`'s hatch-menu path
moved from `nursery_column_wrapper/nursery_column` to
`raises_column_wrapper/raises_column`.

**New fixture.** `test_scenarios/uiw3_cell_tray_columns_test.json`: at
1920x1080, selects a built cell, advances to Challenge 2 stage 2.2, then
asserts `even_columns` finds three equal-width columns in
`cell_primary_columns`, `panel_rect command_tray` matches its content, and
`no_collapsed_controls` holds; then resizes to 800x600 and re-asserts the
panel matches its content there too (columns may wrap at that width).

**Heights recorded, same cell, same fixture path, before/after:** the
pre-change (two-column Nursery) tray's actual rendered height was 165px
(content asked for 181px); the delivered (three-column) tray's actual height
is 151px (content asked for 155px). The split does make the tray shorter, as
Dave asked.

**Settle-drift tolerance.** `_resize_command_tray`'s documented residual
settle drift (`hud.gd`'s `_resettle_command_tray` comment, Dave 10 Sep 2026)
is a few pixels wider here than the code comment's own examples: 16px on the
pre-change layout, 4px after this split (both stable across 20-40 settle
ticks, so not a convergence timing issue -- the drift itself is a pre-existing,
accepted limitation of the settle loop, confirmed by reproducing the larger
16px gap on the unmodified two-column layout with the same fixture shape
before making this change). The new fixture's `matches_content` assertions
use `"tolerance": 5.0` rather than the 1.0px default to absorb this, since
fixing the settle loop itself is out of this slice's scope.

**Regression.** `tools/verify.ps1` (green), the UI-X-2 fixture (green, after
its path update above) and the LAY fixtures (`tests/test_layout_regression.py`,
green).

**Completion.** C-18's cell half moved from `checks/blocked.md` back to
`checks/ready.md`.
