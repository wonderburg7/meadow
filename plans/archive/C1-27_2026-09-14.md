# C1-27 — a Challenge 1 wax cell holds 150 nectar, not 200

**Purpose.** Challenge 1's 1.11 lesson stopped at 150 nectar but a wax cell
still held the shared 200 capacity, so a later trip could raise it to 155.
Every Challenge 1 wax cell must instead be full at 150 nectar, without
changing other modes or resource types.

**Delivered.** `ChallengeStages.C1_NECTAR_CELL_CAPACITY` is 150.0 and
`NECTAR_TARGET` derives from it. `HiveCell` owns a positive,
nectar-only `nectar_capacity_override`, which `capacity_for()` uses before
the resource catalogue and which `_ready()` uses for its initial displayed
capacity. Challenge 1's build-site path applies the override only when
`Session.starting_level == 1`, alongside its existing build-time override.

**Constraints preserved.** `GameConfig.cell_capacity`, Challenge 2 and
open-world cells remain at 200; pollen, honey and brood capacities are
unchanged. The Challenge 1 overflow-discard rule remains limited to stage
1.14.

**Regression proof.** `tests/source_checks.py` verifies the cap constant,
the target's derivation, `capacity_for()`'s nectar override path, and a
Challenge-1-only matching override for every build-time override. The branch
`tools/verify.ps1` suite passed.

**Player checks restored.** C-31 and C-42 moved from blocked to ready with
their actions and pass criteria unchanged.
